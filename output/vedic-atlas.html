  1. Library follows the supplied texts and their indexed contents.
  2. Connections brings together passages that share names or ideas. A shared theme does not establish literary borrowing.

Page numbers always mean PDF pages, starting at 1. Use “Open source PDF” to check the original. Extracted and recognized text may contain errors. Indexed headings are navigation aids; they are not a claim that every embedded story has received a separate scholarly analysis.
